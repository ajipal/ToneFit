# ToneFit — 72 Outfit Generation Prompts

12 color sub-types × 6 styles = 72 images total.

**File naming convention:**
```
outfits/[subtype_key]/[style].jpg
```

**Subtype keys:** `spring_warm` · `spring_light` · `spring_bright` · `summer_cool` · `summer_light` · `summer_soft` · `autumn_warm` · `autumn_soft` · `autumn_deep` · `winter_cool` · `winter_deep` · `winter_bright`

**Style keys:** `casual` · `smart_casual` · `classic` · `streetwear` · `retro` · `formal`

---

## Style Clothing Reference

| Style | Clothing Items |
|---|---|
| Casual | Relaxed lightweight sweater, relaxed-fit wide trousers, casual sneakers, small crossbody bag |
| Smart Casual | Untucked button-down shirt, slim chinos, clean leather loafers, structured tote bag |
| Classic | Structured blazer, tailored wide-leg dress trousers, leather oxford shoes, structured leather handbag |
| Streetwear | Oversized hoodie, relaxed cargo pants, chunky sneakers, small backpack |
