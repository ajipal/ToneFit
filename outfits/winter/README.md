# Winter Season — Outfit Photos

Place sample outfit photos for the **Winter** personal color season here.

## Who is a Winter?
- Undertone: **Cool**
- Skin: Deep, clear, high contrast (cool-toned or neutral-cool)
- Features: High contrast between skin, hair, and eyes
- Examples: Heart Evangelista, Megan Fox, Dua Lipa, Jisoo (BLACKPINK), Keanu Reeves

## What photos to add
Add `.jpg` or `.png` images of outfits that flatter Winter coloring.
Aim for **3–6 photos** across casual, smart casual, and formal styles.

## Color palette to follow
| Category | Colors |
|----------|--------|
| Tops | True white, black, royal blue, crimson, emerald |
| Bottoms | Black, crisp white, deep navy, dark charcoal |
| Outerwear | Black, cobalt, deep magenta, bright white |
| Avoid | Warm brown, mustard, dusty pastels, warm beige |

## Outfit ideas
- **Casual**: Black tee, crisp white jeans, royal blue jacket
- **Smart Casual**: Emerald green top with black trousers, cobalt blazer
- **Formal**: Stark white or black gown, jewel-tone suit

## File naming
Use descriptive names so the team knows what each photo shows:
```
winter_casual_black_royalblue.jpg
winter_smartcasual_emerald_black.jpg
winter_formal_white_gown.jpg
```

## Where photos appear in the app
These images are displayed in the **Outfits tab** of the ToneFit app
when a user is predicted as a Winter season.
